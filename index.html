How a county project moves

  1. PlanningIdentified in public participation, approved and budgeted.
  2. Tender and awardAdvertised, bids evaluated and a contract awarded.
  3. Site handoverThe contractor takes the site; a Project Management Committee of residents oversees it.
  4. WorksConstruction, with inspections and progress updates.
  5. CompletionFinal inspection, commissioning and handover to the community.

      Project grievance desk (GRM)

      If the library project affects you — access, noise, dust, safety, land, workers' conduct or anything else — tell us. It is free, you may stay anonymous, and nobody may punish you for raising a concern.

      1. Report Use this form, email feedback@garissa.go.ke or tell the site office.
      2. Acknowledge You get a reference number immediately.
      3. Review The project grievance committee looks into it.
      4. Reply You receive an answer within 7 days.
      5. Appeal Not satisfied? The County KDSP II coordination unit reviews the case.

      Citizen feedback

      Ask a question, make a complaint, give a compliment or suggest an improvement. Every message gets a reference number and a reply within 7 days.

      Type of feedback

      Your contact details are used only to reply to you and are never published (Data Protection Act, 2019).

      What happens next

      1. Received You see a reference number on screen and, if you gave an email, an acknowledgement in your inbox.
      2. Routed Your message goes to feedback@garissa.go.ke and is assigned to the responsible department.
      3. Answered The County replies within 7 days.
